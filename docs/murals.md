## Mural 1

- **Story (from guest relations):**
- **What it shows:** A woman painted straight onto the brick, with a gold leaf on her forehead, gold hoop earrings, stacked gold neck rings and a robe of patterned panels. Flat-topped trees with pale trunks stand behind her on both sides.
- **Concept (draft):** Tracking finds the wall and the painting slowly wakes up. The trees start to sway from the base of their trunks, the earrings swing from where they hang, and leaves drift down in paint dust. Nothing leaves the wall: it is the painting itself breathing.
- **Interaction 1 (draft):** Tap a tree. A gust runs through it: it bends further, shakes loose a burst of painted leaves, then settles back into its sway.
- **Interaction 2 (draft):** Tap an earring. Both earrings swing out and catch the light with a soft chime, and a gold shimmer runs down through the neck rings.
- **Extra interactions (draft):** Walking sideways shows the trees and earrings separating from the wall (parallax).
- **Audio mood (draft):** Wind through leaves, birds far off, light metallic chimes.
- **Elements to cut out:** done (earring left, earring right, tree right, tree left)

## Mural 2

- **Story (from guest relations):**
- **What it shows:** A woman framed by a large black circle, eyes covered by a crown of flowers, hands raised to it. A map of Africa sits on her purple top, with a beaded collar at her neck and trailing flowers at her side. Pale modernist buildings fill the left of the wall.
- **Concept (draft):** The flowers are the way in. On scan, the flower crown and the flowers at her side begin to grow and open, then a few blooms lift off the wall and float out towards the viewer in 3D, painted in the mural's own colours.
- **Interaction 1 (draft):** Tap a flower. It opens fully, and petals scatter around the user and fall.
- **Interaction 2 (draft):** Tap the beaded collar. The beads light up one by one and the glow travels down into the map of Africa.
- **Extra interactions (draft):** Tap a floating bloom to send it back into the crown.
- **Audio mood (draft):** Warm, slow and hopeful. Soft strings or kalimba, gentle chimes as flowers open.
- **Elements to cut out:** done (flower crown, flowers left, necklace)

## Mural 3

- **Story (from guest relations):**
- **What it shows:** The Mosque of Muhammad Ali in the Cairo Citadel, with its domes and minarets, set in a desert with tall cacti and agave plants in front. The mural continues along the same wall into other scenes, including Great Zimbabwe.
- **Background (researched, confirm with guest relations before using in the app):** The mosque was commissioned by Muhammad Ali Pasha, begun in 1830 and finished in 1857. It is also called the Alabaster Mosque. It has a large central dome and two minarets of about 80 m. Source: [Wikipedia, Muhammad Ali Mosque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ali_Mosque)
- **Concept (draft):** The wall opens into the desert. Sand blows across the bottom of the mural, the sky drifts, the plants peel slightly off the wall and lean in the wind, and the desert floor extends out past the edges of the painting onto the ground in front of the user.
- **Interaction 1 (draft):** Tap the cactus or the plants. A gust of wind sweeps the scene: the plants bend, sand lifts and settles.
- **Interaction 2 (draft):** Tap the mosque. An info hotspot opens with the confirmed facts about the building.
- **Extra interactions (draft):** Hold the phone still and the light on the domes shifts slowly from day to dusk.
- **Audio mood (draft):** Desert wind, distant city sounds, a calm call of birds at dusk.
- **Elements to cut out:** done (plants front, plants left, cactus)

## Mural 4

- **Story (from guest relations):**
- **What it shows:** On the left, a large abstract mask made of curved, interlocking shapes in gold, blue and black. On the right, five painted portraits set into dark landscape panels split by gold lines: a woman wearing a crown, a turbaned man holding a gold sceptre and wearing gold necklaces, a man in a red beret, a man in a dark jacket, and a smiling woman in a cap. The names of the people must come from guest relations.
- **Concept (draft):** A story told one face at a time. The gold lines light up and run like rivers across the wall, and each portrait in turn lifts forward off the wall, holds for a moment, and settles back as the light moves on to the next.
- **Interaction 1 (draft):** Tap a portrait. It lifts towards the user and the info panel shows that person's name and story (only once confirmed by guest relations).
- **Interaction 2 (draft):** Trace or tap along the gold lines. Light flows along them and connects the portraits to the mask.
- **Extra interactions (draft):** Once all five portraits have been opened, the mask's eyes light up and the experience completes.
- **Audio mood (draft):** Drums and a low choir, building as each portrait is revealed.
- **Elements to cut out:** done (portrait crown, portrait turban, portrait beret, portrait right, portrait cap)

## Mural 5

- **Story (from guest relations):**
- **What it shows:** A bold graphic figure with gold rings orbiting her head like a headdress, arms raised with stacked gold bangles, a second face on the right, all set against rays of teal, coral, black and white.
- **Concept (draft):** The rings come apart and rebuild in 3D. On scan, the painted rings lift off the wall and become real 3D rings that start orbiting the figure's head, while the rays behind her pulse gently.
- **Interaction 1 (draft):** Tap the rings. They spin faster and spread out, then slow back down and settle into orbit.
- **Interaction 2 (draft):** Tap the bangles. They slide down the arm with a clink and send a pulse of light out along the rays.
- **Extra interactions (draft):** Tap the rings when they are lined up to stack them back into the painting.
- **Audio mood (draft):** Upbeat electronic rhythm with metallic percussion that matches the bangle taps.
- **Elements to cut out:** done (rings, bangles raised, bangles low)
